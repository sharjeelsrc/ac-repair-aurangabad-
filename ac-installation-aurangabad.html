Our Installation Process

How We Install Your AC in Aurangabad

SR Enterprises follows a professional 6-step installation process for every AC in Aurangabad — ensuring safe, efficient and long-lasting results.

📐

Step 1 — Site Survey

Technician visits to assess wall strength, pipe routing, electrical capacity and best position for indoor and outdoor units in your Aurangabad home.

🔩

Step 2 — Wall Mounting

Heavy-duty brackets are drilled and secured. Indoor unit mounted at optimal height — minimum 7 feet — for maximum cooling coverage.

🔗

Step 3 — Copper Piping

ISI-certified copper pipes are laid between indoor and outdoor units. Pipes are insulated to prevent condensation and heat loss.

⚡

Step 4 — Electrical Wiring

Safe electrical connection with MCB protection. Correct wire gauge used based on AC tonnage. All wiring done per IS code standards.

🧊

Step 5 — Gas Pressure Test

After piping, refrigerant pressure is verified to ensure no leaks before the system is charged and tested for correct gas levels.

✅

Step 6 — Test Run

Full test run performed — cooling checked, temperature verified, drainage confirmed. You receive a 90-day warranty card at job completion.
