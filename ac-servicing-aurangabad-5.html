in Aurangabad

Aurangabad summers are extreme — regular AC servicing by SR Enterprises ensures your AC performs at 100% when you need it most, saving you money on electricity and repairs.

Reduces Electricity Bill

A clean, well-serviced AC consumes 10–20% less electricity. In Aurangabad's long summers, this saves ₹500–₹2000 per month on your electricity bill.

Better Cooling Performance

Dirty coils and clogged filters reduce cooling by up to 30%. After SR Enterprises jet pump servicing your AC cools faster and reaches set temperature quicker.

Regular servicing reduces strain on the compressor and fan motor — the two most expensive parts. A serviced AC lasts 12–15 years vs 7–8 years unserviced.
