Washing Machine Repair in Aurangabad & Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Short answer: SR Enterprises provides doorstep washing machine diagnosis and repair across Aurangabad and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. The service covers front-load, top-load, fully automatic and semi-automatic machines. Common jobs include no-spin, drainage, water-inlet, leakage, vibration, door-lock, PCB and error-code faults.

What we actually check

A washing machine symptom does not always identify the failed part. A no-spin complaint, for example, can involve drainage, door locking, motor drive, belt, capacitor on applicable models, wiring or electronic control. Our approach is to identify the fault first rather than recommend a part simply because of the symptom.

  • Power supply, wiring and safety interlocks
  • Water inlet, drain path and pump
  • Motor, belt, drum and suspension components
  • Sensors, door lock and control electronics
  • Error codes and PCB-related faults

When to stop using the machine

Switch the machine off and arrange inspection if you notice burning smell, smoke, repeated electrical tripping, exposed wiring, a major water leak or unusual mechanical noise. Do not continue running a machine that may create an electrical or water-safety risk.

Why is my washing machine not spinning?▾
Possible causes include an unbalanced load, drainage problem, door-lock issue, belt or motor fault, capacitor on applicable models, wiring fault or electronic-control problem. Diagnosis is needed before replacing a part.
Why is my washing machine not draining water?▾
Possible causes include a blocked filter or hose, drain-pump fault, wiring issue or control fault. The drain system should be inspected before replacing the pump.
Why is my washing machine leaking water?▾
A leak may come from the door seal, inlet or drain hose, detergent drawer, pump area, tub connection or another seal. The technician should locate the actual leak before deciding what to replace.
Why is my washing machine making loud noise or vibrating?▾
Some vibration can come from an unbalanced load or installation. Persistent grinding, knocking or excessive movement may indicate a mechanical problem such as bearings, suspension or drum-related components.
